## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 117 implements the **Institutional Analytics & Operations Dashboards UI** for the E-WITNESS digital chain-of-custody platform. Building upon the Phase 115 Case Management Dossier and Phase 116 Video Review & AI Annotation Subsystems, this milestone delivers an institutional, role-guarded analytical intelligence environment.

The system surfaces real-time incident reporting trends, case lifecycle statistics, lead investigator capacity allocations, and AI decision oversight telemetry directly backed by the verified backend contracts established in Batch 10 (Phases 95, 96, 97, 98, and 99).

### Core Safety Invariants Enforced
- **Zero Facial Recognition or Biometric Surveillance:** `BIOMETRIC_MATCHING_ENABLED = False` permanently enforced across all layers. Zero facial detection, 1:N biometric searches, suspect clustering, or facial identity recognition is executed or exposed.
- **Strict Machine Non-Authoritativeness:** Every AI metrics card and candidate detection overview prominently features the statutory advisory disclosure: *"AI candidate annotations are non-authoritative machine hypotheses generated by computer vision models. Detections do not establish guilt, prove misconduct, or substitute for human judicial evaluation."*
- **Truthful Data Boundaries & No Hardcoded Samples:** If a metric is not aggregated by the backend (such as an ad-hoc aggregate evidence scanner status endpoint), the dashboard does not fabricate mock data. Metrics strictly mirror backend Pydantic schemas. Missing historical timestamps are rendered as unpopulated rather than assumed zeroes.
- **Stale Request Cancellation & Optimistic Isolation:** Asynchronous dashboard queries use monotonic sequence counters to guarantee stale responses never overwrite newer filter ranges.
- **Role and Department Scoping:** Scoped query parameters subordinate all metrics to the authenticated user's department unless the caller holds `ROLE_SUPER_ADMIN` with global authorization.

### 2.2 Analytics API Client (`frontend/src/api/analytics.ts`)
- `getAnalyticsOverview(filter)`: Queries `GET /analytics/overview` returning unified cross-domain summary.
- `getIncidentAnalytics(filter)`: Queries `GET /analytics/incidents` returning volume histograms and category/severity/status breakdowns.
- `getCaseAnalytics(filter)`: Queries `GET /analytics/cases` returning case creation trends, status distributions, and active/closed caseloads.
- `getReviewOutcomeAnalytics(filter)`: Queries `GET /analytics/reviews` returning human acceptance/rejection rates and documented false-positive categorizations.
- `getReviewerWorkload(departmentId)`: Queries `GET /analytics/workload` returning investigator caseloads, pending reviews, and unassigned queues.
- `getProcessingMetrics(filter)`: Queries `GET /analytics/processing-metrics` returning AI video jobs throughput, p95 processing latency, queue wait times, and delivery success rates.
- `getIncidentDashboard(filter)`: Queries `GET /dashboards/incidents` returning quick operational totals and total evidence items.
- `getCaseDashboard(filter)`: Queries `GET /dashboards/cases` returning active investigations and legal hold counts.
- `getOperationsDashboard(filter)`: Queries `GET /dashboards/operations` returning notification channels, delivery attempts, and failures.
- `getDetailedSystemHealth()`: Queries `GET /health/system` returning deep worker heartbeats, queue depths, and operational alerts.
- `exportReport(payload)`: Submits `POST /reports/export` with authenticated stream download, generating macro-sanitized CSV or PDF reports.
